04 · Product definition and positioning

A connected operating workspace and direct-booking system.

Bookglow helps appointment-led beauty and wellness businesses publish a customer booking link and run the daily work that follows: schedule, customer records, checkout, services/products/packages, staff, vouchers, sales and expenses.

Customer promise

Book the right service, professional and time directly with the merchant through a clear mobile-first experience.

Merchant promise

Keep the appointment, customer, staff, sale and business records connected in one operating workspace.

Platform promise

Provide merchant access, onboarding, product guidance and governance without pretending unsupported commercial infrastructure already exists.

Launch-market language

Beauty salonsSpasMassageNailsBarbersBeauty servicesWellness teamsMalaysia-first
Expansion rule: keep entity and UI architecture reusable for other appointment businesses, but do not weaken launch messaging by trying to speak to every industry now.
05 · One ecosystem, four surfaces

Each surface has a different user, but all share the same product truth.

A · Platform websiteExplains Bookglow, supports merchant discovery, signup/login entry, pricing state, trust and onboarding entry.
B · Merchant portalRuns the business: dashboard, schedule, POS, CRM, catalog, staff, reporting, finance, marketing, settings.
C · Customer bookingDirect merchant storefront and booking journey at /book/:bookingPath.
D · Platform administrationInternal outlet/workspace access control, super-admin oversight and future subscription governance.
The public website acquires merchants. The portal configures and runs the business. The booking link converts customers. Platform administration governs access.

11 · Current Firebase and future Supabase boundary

UI first now; backend replacement later.

Current runtimeFirebase Auth, Firestore subscriptions/services, Cloud Functions, Storage and Firebase multisite hosting.
Prepared migrationmigration/sql, export/import and validation tools exist but are not active application services.
Future runtimeSupabase Auth, PostgreSQL, RLS, transactions/RPC/Edge Functions and appropriate Realtime usage.
During UI upgradeDuring Supabase migration
Preserve existing Firebase calls, listeners, IDs, props and workflows.Replace the data/service boundary behind stable UI contracts.
Do not add new Firebase-only architecture.Map every legacy record and relationship explicitly.
Keep data access out of newly extracted presentation components.Use idempotent provisioning, booking and financial operations.
Do not block UI approval on database work.Run dual-read/validation or controlled cutover as designed later.
The active service file currently declares Firestore as the only provider. The Books must never describe Supabase as already live.
12 · Shared contracts and boundaries

Share contracts before sharing entire app internals.

  • Keep each app independently buildable and deployable.
  • Do not import private files directly from another app’s src or page tree.
  • Extract shared tokens, UI primitives, domain types and analytics only when two surfaces genuinely use them.
  • Keep public booking contracts versioned and merchant-safe.
  • Keep role/permission and route ownership explicit.
  • Keep price, duration, timezone, currency and status formatting consistent.
Recommended gradual shape: retain apps/customer-site and apps/merchant-portal; later add small packages/design-system, packages/contracts and packages/analytics only after current duplication is mapped.
13 · Platform administration

Govern workspaces and access before pretending to operate subscriptions.

NowNextLater
List outlets/workspaces, inspect state, enable/disable portal access and remote-view where supported.Review onboarding status, ownership, booking path, support state and access history.Real plans, subscriptions, entitlements, invoices and payment-provider reconciliation.
Naming rule: “Subscribers” must not imply paid recurring subscribers while the system only represents outlets or access records. The route may remain temporarily, but the UI label should become Outlets, Workspaces or Access.
14 · Commercial wording

Do not design a fictional billing product.

Allowed now

Start free, Request access, Join the pilot, Contact us, and clearly marked provisional pricing.

Not allowed

Live trial countdown, card collection, recurring billing, invoices, feature entitlements or guaranteed plan limits without implementation.

Future decision pack

RM price, billing frequency, plan count, trial, outlet/staff limits, taxes, provider, refund/cancellation and access behavior.

17 · Book 1 acceptance criteria

The ecosystem blueprint is complete only when these remain true.

  • Every active surface has a named user, purpose and route owner.
  • No new V2 application is created.
  • No Fresha-style marketplace is introduced.
  • Launch messaging stays Malaysia beauty/wellness focused.
  • Current Firebase state and future Supabase target are described honestly.
  • Pricing remains provisional until approved.
  • Merchant access supports self-service plus platform governance.
  • References A–C are translated into real Bookglow components rather than copied visually.
  • Books 2 and 3 can be implemented without contradicting Book 1.
18 · Deferred decisions that must not be invented

These can wait, but they must stay explicit.

Commercial

Final RM pricing, plans, trial, billing provider, taxes and entitlements.

Brand

Final production logo assets, exact tagline, photography library and domain strategy.

Booking policy

Deposits, online payments, cancellation windows, rescheduling limits and no-show rules.

Operations

Support SLA, merchant approval criteria, onboarding assistance and account suspension policy.

Growth

Verified testimonials, integrations, campaign channels and evidence claims.

Migration

Supabase cutover date, dual-run strategy, data ownership and archival policy.

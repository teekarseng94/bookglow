Customer Booking Upgrade Blueprint

The complete direct-merchant booking blueprint for refining Bookglow’s live customer journey across storefront, service, professional, availability, details, review and confirmation.

Make the merchant feel credible, each decision feel simple, and the booking result feel certain.

01 · Mandate and booking truth

Upgrade the live direct-booking route, not a parallel customer application.

Book 3 governs apps/customer-site, especially /book/:bookingPath. The current project already includes a redesigned booking page, merchant header, service cards, sticky action and governed state components. The next implementation phase audits and completes that live flow rather than reviving an experimental /book-v2 route.

Model

Direct merchant booking link. No public marketplace, nearby search or merchant-discovery feed.

Customer goal

Understand the merchant, choose service/professional/time, provide details, review and receive certainty.

Merchant goal

Publish a credible storefront that reflects real services, staff, hours, availability and policies.

The booking experience belongs to the merchant’s brand, but every choice and recovery path is governed by Bookglow.

03 · Canonical customer journey

Seven clear stages with persistent context.

01Merchant storefront and service discoveryIdentity, location, hours, categories, services
02Service selectionName, description, duration, price
03Professional preferenceAny available or qualified named professional
04Date and timeAvailable dates, slots and alternatives
05Customer detailsMinimal contact, optional notes, account convenience
06Review and confirmMerchant, appointment, customer and price
07Confirmation and managementReference, calendar, directions, contact and future manage link

Customers may navigate backward without losing valid choices. A changed service may invalidate professional and slot; a changed professional may invalidate slot; changing contact details should not clear appointment choices.

04 · Routes and direct-link behavior

The merchant link is the customer’s entry point.

RoutePurposeRequirement
/book/:bookingPathLive merchant storefront and bookingbookingPath resolves a supported outlet ID or booking slug.
/book/:bookingPath/authCustomer login/signupReturn to the same merchant booking route after success.
/signupPublic signup entrySeparate merchant signup intent from customer booking authentication.

*Platform landingReference A and Book 1 govern this experience.
No marketplace rule: Bookglow does not add routes for search, nearby merchants, categories across merchants or public ranking. Merchants distribute their own booking link through website, QR, social, chat or direct sharing.

07 · Professional selection

Flexibility first, identity second.

Any available

First, recommended option. Explain that Bookglow will assign a qualified available professional.

Named professional

Photo/initial, name, role or concise descriptor, with a clear selected state.

Qualification

Only professionals eligible for the chosen service appear. Do not expose internal qualification rules.

ChangeDependency behavior
Change serviceRe-evaluate professionals and clear an invalid professional/slot.
Change professionalRe-fetch/recalculate availability and clear an invalid slot.
Select any availableAvailability represents qualified staff pool and final assignment is server-safe in the backend phase.

09 · Customer details and authentication

Do not turn booking into an account-creation obstacle.

Required

Name and the contact method Bookglow needs to confirm and operate the appointment.

Optional

Email, notes and future convenience fields according to merchant policy.

Authentication

Customer may log in or create a profile for convenience, but returns to the same merchant and retained booking context.

  • Visible labels and meaningful autocomplete.
  • Malaysia-friendly phone entry with country context.
  • Inline validation after interaction or submit.
  • Mobile keyboard never covers the primary action.
  • Privacy/support copy explains how contact details are used.
  • Customer role never redirects into the merchant portal.

BookingAuth.tsx and booking-specific auth helpers remain distinct from merchant-oriented registration helpers.

10 · Review and confirmation submission

One controlled final action.

Review groupContent and edit behavior
MerchantName and outlet/location; returns to storefront context if needed.
AppointmentService, duration, professional preference, date and time; edit returns to the relevant stage.
CustomerName and confirmation contact; edit does not clear appointment.
Price/policyPrice and any supported deposit/cancellation wording.
  • Confirm button has a precise label and locks against duplicate clicks.
  • Submitting state preserves the full review.
  • Known failure returns a specific recovery path.
  • Unknown result does not invite immediate duplicate booking.
  • Slot conflict returns to time selection with other details preserved.
11 · Confirmation and aftercare

The customer must leave with proof and next steps.

Appointment proof

Status, reference, merchant, service, professional, date, time, duration and price.

Useful actions

Add to calendar, directions, contact merchant, book another and future manage booking.

Communication

Where confirmation was sent and what to do if it does not arrive.

Do not use a giant success icon as the page’s main content. Appointment details and next actions are the confirmation experience.
12 · Booking management boundary

Management is part of the intended journey, but must be secure.

CapabilityUX directionStatus
View bookingSecure token or authenticated ownership; show full appointment and merchant details.Later backend
CancelPolicy, reason, confirmation and resulting status.Later backend
RescheduleReuse service/professional context, show new availability, atomic update.Later backend
Contact merchantAvailable now when contact data is configured.UI now

Do not create a public appointment lookup based on guessable IDs. Until secure management exists, confirmation wording must not promise it.

13 · Governed booking states

Every failure and empty state protects customer confidence.

StateRequired message and recovery
Merchant not foundThe link may be invalid or expired; do not expose internal outlet identifiers.
Merchant inactiveOnline booking is unavailable; show contact only if configured.
No servicesMerchant has no online services; offer contact/retry.
Service unavailableChoose another service.
No qualified professionalChoose another service or contact merchant.
No slotsNearby dates, next available or any professional.
Load failureRetry while retaining the merchant link.
Submission failureSpecific retry or return path; retain all safe form state.
Unknown submissionCheck/reconcile before allowing another confirm.
Missing imageNeutral branded fallback.

Use BookingStateScreen, BookingEmptyState and centralised bookingMessages.ts to prevent inconsistent wording.

16 · Accessibility and trust

A booking flow must work without perfect vision, precision or connectivity.

  • WCAG 2.2 AA colour contrast and focus visibility.
  • 44 px touch targets for primary controls and time slots.
  • Semantic buttons, inputs, headings and progress labels.
  • Screen-reader announcements for selection, loading and booking result.
  • Reduced-motion support.
  • Error text is associated with the correct field.
  • Time, date, currency and phone formats are understandable in the Malaysia launch context.
  • Back navigation and browser refresh do not produce unsafe duplicate actions.

22 · Book 3 completion definition

The booking upgrade is complete when customers can finish confidently on any supported device.

  • The live /book/:bookingPath route is the only canonical booking journey.
  • Merchant context remains visible without delaying service discovery.
  • Service, professional, date/time, details, review and confirmation are coherent.
  • Mobile sticky action and desktop summary always reflect current valid selections.
  • Backward editing preserves compatible data and clears incompatible dependencies.
  • All loading, empty, inactive, conflict and failure states have safe recovery.
  • Customer auth returns to the same merchant journey.
  • No marketplace is introduced.
  • Firebase behavior is preserved during UI work; Supabase remains a later migration.

02 · Approved decisions

Decisions that must not be reopened during implementation without owner approval.

  • Upgrade the existing Bookglow monorepo; do not build a separate V2 app.
  • Firebase remains the current runtime during UI work; Supabase is the later migration target.
  • No Fresha-style public marketplace.
  • Launch market is Malaysia beauty and wellness.
  • Pricing remains provisional.
  • Merchant entry supports self-service plus super-admin governance.
  • Bright Fresha-inspired polish and Setmore-inspired platform clarity.
